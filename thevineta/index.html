Retrofit what you have. Replace only what is done.

PTACs. VRFs. Kitchen hoods. Pool equipment. A new system still has waste in it. An old one still has life in it. We match the fix to the machine: more output, less service, and the gold standard only when a unit is finished.

  • For any refrigerant circuit · PTACs and VRFs alike

    The coil treatment

    Compressor oil bakes onto the inside of the coil circuit. A wash never reaches it. One refrigerant-side treatment lifts it off, for the life of the unit — including one that is new.

    Recovers up to 36% of lost performance1, 12

  • For simple on/off compressors · most PTACs

    The HVAC optimizer

    Learns how the room actually runs and shuts the compressor off the moment it stops doing useful work, while the fan keeps delivering the stored comfort. Nothing changes at the pillow.

    15–30% less HVAC energy1

  • For inverter-type systems · VRFs

    The solar coil

    Not a solar panel: a solar thermal retrofit that captures the sun's heat and puts it to work for the compressor. Sits with the equipment, not on the historic façade.

    30–50% more efficient system1

  • For the panels that feed hoods, pumps, and compressors

    Power conditioning

    Kitchen hoods and pool equipment do not draw power smoothly. Distortion becomes heat. Heat retires a machine early — and it fattens the peak. We clean the power that reaches them.

    A quieter peak, longer life1, 12

  • For the public rooms · courtyard, Coco, Pool House

    Dehumidification

    The keys sit at 72. The courtyard never dries. Taking moisture out at the source is cheaper than asking every guest-room compressor to fight Palm Beach dew point all year.

    Moisture handled once, not in every key1, 12
